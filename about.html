Who We Are

Cliva India is a growing fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) company based in Gurugram, Haryana. With a legacy of over 3.5 years, our journey reflects our focus on building a diverse consumer product portfolio and exploring opportunities across the Indian FMCG landscape.

Everyday products play an important role in the lives of consumers. From the beverages people enjoy to the snacks they share and the essentials they use at home, these products are part of everyday routines. At Cliva India, this understanding shapes the way we look at our product categories and business opportunities.

Our portfolio spans packaged drinking water, beverages, namkeen and snacks, spices and masala, cookies, and home hygiene products. Bringing these diverse categories together gives our brand a broader presence across everyday consumer requirements.

We believe that a strong FMCG business is built through a combination of market understanding, relevant product choices and dependable business relationships. We value the role of retailers, distributors, wholesalers and institutional buyers in connecting products with consumers.

As we continue our journey, our focus remains on developing the Cliva India brand, strengthening our product portfolio and exploring meaningful opportunities within the fast-moving consumer goods industry.
