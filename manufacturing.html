The Process

11 Stages of the Manufacturing Journey

A step-by-step overview of the activities that may form part of a product's journey from requirements to dispatch.

STAGE 01

Requirement Planning

Understanding product requirements, specifications and planned quantities.

STAGE 02

Material Sourcing

Identifying and arranging the materials and packaging inputs required for production.

STAGE 03

Incoming Material Checks

Reviewing incoming materials against applicable requirements and specifications.

STAGE 04

Production Preparation

Preparing the production area, equipment and relevant process requirements.

STAGE 05

Processing & Production

Carrying out the relevant product manufacturing or processing activities.

STAGE 06

In-Process Checks

Monitoring relevant process parameters and product requirements during production.

STAGE 07

Quality Inspection

Reviewing product quality against applicable checks and acceptance criteria.

STAGE 08

Filling & Packaging

Filling, packing or preparing finished products in the relevant packaging format.

STAGE 09

Labelling & Identification

Applying relevant product labels, identification and packaging information.

STAGE 10

Finished Goods Storage

Organising finished products for storage and subsequent order processing.

STAGE 11

Order Dispatch

Preparing and coordinating finished product orders for dispatch and delivery.
