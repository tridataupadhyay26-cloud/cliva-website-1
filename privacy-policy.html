1. INTRODUCTION

Cliva India ("Cliva", "Company", "we", "our" or "us") recognises the importance of protecting the privacy and personal information of individuals who visit, access or interact with our website and business communication platforms.

We are committed to maintaining transparency, confidentiality and responsible data handling practices throughout our business operations. This Privacy Policy describes the types of information we collect, the purposes for which such information is used, the circumstances under which it may be disclosed, and the measures adopted to protect personal information.

This Policy applies to all individuals and organisations interacting with Cliva India, including website visitors, customers, prospective customers, distributors, dealers, retailers, suppliers, vendors and business associates.

By accessing or using our website, submitting an enquiry or voluntarily sharing information with us, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy, subject to applicable consent and other legal requirements.

2. SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, use, storage, disclosure and protection of personal information obtained through:

  • The official Cliva India website and associated web pages.
  • Online contact forms and product enquiry forms.
  • Email correspondence and business communication.
  • Customer support and sales-related interactions.
  • Distributor, dealer and business partnership enquiries.
  • Marketing, promotional and informational communication.
  • Other authorised digital or electronic communication channels.

This Policy applies to personal information processed by or on behalf of Cliva India in connection with its website and related business activities.

Information collected through independent third-party platforms may also be subject to the respective privacy policies and terms of those platforms.

3. CATEGORIES OF INFORMATION COLLECTED

Cliva India may collect different categories of information depending on the nature of the interaction, services requested and business relationship.

3.1 Personal Identification Information

This may include:

  • Full name and contact details.
  • Email address and mobile number.
  • Residential or business contact information, where voluntarily provided.
  • Professional designation and organisational details.
  • Other identification information voluntarily submitted through authorised communication channels.

3.2 Business and Commercial Information

For commercial enquiries and business relationships, we may collect:

  • Company or organisation name.
  • Business address and operating location.
  • Nature and category of business.
  • Distribution and dealership requirements.
  • Product categories and purchase preferences.
  • Estimated order quantities and commercial requirements.
  • Supplier, retailer or wholesale-related information.
  • Relevant business correspondence and transaction-related details.

3.3 Technical and Electronic Information

When visitors access our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Operating system and device information.
  • Website access timestamps.
  • Pages visited and navigation patterns.
  • Referral websites and traffic sources.
  • Session information and website interaction data.
  • Technical diagnostic and performance information.

3.4 Communication and Enquiry Information

We may maintain records of enquiries, feedback, requests, complaints and correspondence received through email, telephone, online forms or other authorised communication channels.

Such records may be used for customer service, business coordination, internal administration and dispute resolution.

4. METHODS OF INFORMATION COLLECTION

Information may be collected through direct, automatic and other lawful means.

Direct Collection: Information voluntarily provided by visitors when submitting website forms, requesting product details, communicating with our sales team or expressing interest in business opportunities.

Automatic Collection: Certain technical information may be collected through cookies, server logs, analytics tools and similar website technologies during normal website usage.

Business Communication: Information received through customer interactions, distributor enquiries, supplier discussions and other commercial communications.

Third-Party Sources: Where legally permitted, limited business-related information may be received from authorised business partners, service providers or publicly available sources for legitimate business purposes.

Cliva India endeavours to ensure that information collected is relevant, proportionate and reasonably necessary for the stated purposes.

5. PURPOSE AND USE OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

Information collected by Cliva India may be processed for specific, legitimate and lawful business purposes.

5.1 Customer Enquiry Management

To receive, review and respond to enquiries relating to our products, pricing, availability, packaging, specifications and other business requirements.

5.2 Product and Service Information

To provide relevant information about Cliva India's product portfolio, business offerings, product categories and associated commercial services.

5.3 Distribution and Dealership Management

To evaluate and respond to enquiries from prospective distributors, dealers, wholesalers, retailers and other commercial partners.

5.4 Business Relationship Management

To establish, maintain and manage relationships with customers, suppliers, distributors, vendors and other business associates.

5.5 Marketing and Promotional Activities

To communicate product launches, promotional activities, business announcements, company updates and other relevant information, where legally permitted and subject to applicable consent requirements.

5.6 Website Improvement and Analytics

To monitor website performance, analyse visitor interactions, improve website navigation, identify technical issues and enhance the overall browsing experience.

5.7 Security and Fraud Prevention

To protect the website, business infrastructure and communication systems against fraudulent activities, unauthorised access, misuse and potential security threats.

5.8 Legal and Regulatory Compliance

To maintain business records, respond to lawful requests, comply with statutory requirements and protect the legal rights and interests of Cliva India.

6. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING AND CONSENT

Cliva India processes personal information in accordance with applicable Indian privacy and data protection laws.

Depending on the nature and purpose of processing, information may be processed based on consent, legitimate permitted uses or other lawful grounds recognised under applicable legislation.

Where consent is required, Cliva India will endeavour to ensure that consent is:

  • Free, informed and specific.
  • Based on a clear understanding of the purpose of processing.
  • Obtained through an affirmative action.
  • Capable of being withdrawn through appropriate channels.

The withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing undertaken before such withdrawal.

Where processing is permitted under another lawful basis, consent may not be the applicable requirement.

7. SHARING AND DISCLOSURE OF INFORMATION

Cliva India respects the confidentiality of personal information and does not sell, rent, lease or commercially trade personal information to third parties.

However, information may be disclosed in certain limited circumstances.

7.1 Authorised Service Providers

Information may be shared with website hosting companies, IT infrastructure providers, analytics platforms, email service providers and other technology partners engaged to support our business operations.

7.2 Internal Business Operations

Relevant information may be accessed by authorised employees or representatives responsible for customer enquiries, sales, distribution, business development and administrative functions.

7.3 Legal and Regulatory Authorities

Information may be disclosed to government authorities, regulatory bodies, law enforcement agencies or courts where disclosure is required or permitted under applicable laws.

7.4 Business Restructuring

In the event of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, sale or transfer of business assets, relevant information may be transferred subject to applicable legal requirements and appropriate confidentiality safeguards.

7.5 Protection of Legal Rights

Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, address security concerns, enforce legal rights or protect the interests of Cliva India and other stakeholders.

All such disclosures will be limited to information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

8. DATA SECURITY AND CONFIDENTIALITY

Cliva India considers information security an important part of its business and website operations.

We endeavour to implement reasonable technical, organisational and administrative safeguards designed to protect personal information against:

  • Unauthorised access or disclosure.
  • Accidental loss or destruction.
  • Unauthorised alteration or modification.
  • Misuse or fraudulent processing.
  • Data theft and security vulnerabilities.
  • Other unlawful or unauthorised activities.

Access to personal information is intended to be restricted to authorised personnel and service providers who require such information for legitimate business purposes.

Although reasonable security measures are adopted, no electronic transmission, online platform or storage infrastructure can guarantee complete protection against every possible threat. Therefore, absolute data security cannot be guaranteed.

9. COOKIES AND TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES

The Cliva India website may use cookies, web beacons, pixels and similar technologies to improve functionality and understand visitor interactions.

Cookies are small data files stored on a visitor's device that may help websites recognise returning visitors and remember certain preferences.

These technologies may be used for:

  • Improving website functionality and navigation.
  • Remembering user preferences.
  • Understanding visitor behaviour and traffic patterns.
  • Analysing website performance.
  • Identifying technical errors.
  • Enhancing website content and user experience.

Visitors may manage or disable cookies through browser settings or available website controls.

Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality and the availability of certain features.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate notice and consent will be obtained before using non-essential cookies or tracking technologies.

10. DATA STORAGE, RETENTION AND DELETION

Cliva India retains personal information only for the period reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected or processed.

Retention periods may vary depending on:

  • The nature and sensitivity of the information.
  • The purpose for which information was collected.
  • The duration of the relevant business relationship.
  • Customer service and communication requirements.
  • Applicable accounting, commercial and statutory requirements.
  • Legal proceedings, dispute resolution and regulatory obligations.

Upon completion of the relevant purpose, information will be securely deleted, erased or anonymised where legally permissible.

Certain records may be retained for longer periods where required by law or necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

Subject to applicable laws, individuals may have certain rights regarding their personal information.

11.1 Right to Access Information

Individuals may request access to information concerning their personal data and relevant details about its processing, to the extent provided under applicable law.

11.2 Right to Correction

Individuals may request the correction or updating of inaccurate, incomplete or outdated personal information.

11.3 Right to Erasure

Individuals may request the erasure of personal information where permitted under applicable law and where continued retention is not legally necessary.

11.4 Right to Withdraw Consent

Where personal information is processed based on consent, individuals may withdraw such consent through the designated communication channels.

11.5 Right to Grievance Redressal

Individuals may raise concerns or complaints regarding the collection, processing, disclosure or protection of personal information.

Where applicable under Indian data protection legislation, individuals may exercise their right to nominate another person to exercise relevant rights in the event of death or incapacity.

Requests will be reviewed and addressed in accordance with applicable laws and prescribed procedures.

Cliva India may use contact information to share relevant product information, promotional announcements, company updates and business communications.

Such communications will be undertaken in accordance with applicable consent requirements and privacy laws.

16. DATA BREACH MANAGEMENT

In the event of a suspected or confirmed personal data breach, Cliva India will take reasonable measures to investigate, assess and contain the incident.

Depending on the nature and severity of the incident, appropriate corrective actions may include:

  • Identifying and addressing the source of the incident.
  • Restricting unauthorised access.
