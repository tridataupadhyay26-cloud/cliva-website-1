ABOUT CLIVA INDIA

Bringing Everyday Essentials Closer to You.

Cliva India is a growing FMCG company based in Gurugram, Haryana, bringing together a diverse range of everyday consumer products. We aim to make daily essentials accessible through products that connect with the changing needs and preferences of consumers.

Our product portfolio covers multiple everyday categories, including packaged drinking water, bottled water, beverages, namkeen, snacks, spices, cookies and home hygiene products. By bringing these categories together, Cliva aims to create convenient choices for consumers and business partners.

We believe that strong business relationships, dependable product availability and a customer-focused approach are important to building a growing FMCG brand. We aim to develop meaningful partnerships with retailers, distributors and businesses while expanding our presence across product categories.

From everyday hydration to food and household essentials, Cliva India is focused on bringing a variety of products together under one growing brand.
